## SECTION 8 — How I Work
A 6-step visual:
1. Discovery call (free, 20 min)
2. Mutual NDA signed
3. Access granted to QBO/Xero + bank statements
4. Diagnostic + 30-day plan delivered
5. Monthly close + 1-page summary email every month
6. Quarterly review call to plan ahead

- *Do you handle taxes?* — I prepare clean books for your tax preparer. I don't file taxes myself (not licensed in your country).
