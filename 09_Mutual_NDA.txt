MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered
into as of [DATE] (the "Effective Date") between:

  PARTY A: [Client Legal Name]
  Address: [Client Address]

  PARTY B: [Your Legal Name]
  Address: [Your City, Philippines]

(Each, a "Party" and together, the "Parties.")

1. PURPOSE
The Parties wish to share certain Confidential Information to
evaluate or perform a remote bookkeeping engagement.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information
disclosed by one Party to the other, in any form, including but
not limited to: financial records, bank statements, credit card
data, tax filings, customer and vendor lists, pricing, payroll
data, login credentials, intellectual property, and business plans.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
   a) is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement;
   b) was already known to the receiving Party before disclosure;
   c) is independently developed by the receiving Party;
   d) is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY
The receiving Party agrees to:
   a) Hold Confidential Information in strict confidence;
   b) Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose stated above;
   c) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent;
   d) Apply the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential information (and no less than reasonable care);
   e) Restrict access to those personnel who need it for the Purpose.

5. DATA SECURITY STANDARDS
The receiving Party agrees to:
   a) Use unique passwords stored in a reputable password manager;
   b) Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all accounts holding Confidential Information;
   c) Use full-disk encryption on devices accessing Confidential Information;
   d) Never transmit credentials via plain email or unencrypted messaging;
   e) Promptly notify the other Party of any actual or suspected breach.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement remains in effect for [3] years from the Effective Date.
Confidentiality obligations under Section 4 survive termination for
[3] years following termination.

7. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION
Upon termination or written request, the receiving Party will return
or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession and certify
destruction in writing.

8. NO LICENSE
Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license, right,
title, or interest in the other Party's intellectual property.

9. NO OBLIGATION TO ENGAGE
This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into a
business relationship.

10. REMEDIES
The Parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement may cause
irreparable harm and agree that the non-breaching Party may seek
equitable relief in addition to other remedies available at law.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement will be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction —
typically the Client's country or state] without regard to its
conflict-of-laws principles.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement is the complete and exclusive understanding between
the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior
discussions.
