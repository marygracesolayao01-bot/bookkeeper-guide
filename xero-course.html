01

Organization Setup

Create the Xero org. Set country (AU/NZ/UK), financial year end, default currency, time zone. Upload logo. Add organization description. Set the conversion date — the start date of bookkeeping in Xero. Conversion balances must equal trial balance from the previous system on this exact date.

02

Chart of Accounts (COA)

Xero ships with a default COA per country. Customize it. Account types: Revenue, Other Income, Direct Costs, Expense, Current Asset, Fixed Asset, Inventory, Non-current Asset, Current Liability, Non-current Liability, Equity. Account codes follow a country pattern (AU: 200s revenue, 300s direct costs, 400s expense, 600s asset, 800s liability, 900s equity). Mark accounts as 'Show on Dashboard Watchlist' for the owner.

03

Users & Permissions

User roles: Adviser (full access + reports), Standard (data entry), Read Only, Invoice Only, Payroll Admin. Add Two-Step Authentication. NEVER share login credentials with clients — always invite them with appropriate role. For accountants: connect via Xero HQ or Xero Practice Manager (XPM).

04

Conversion Balances

Settings → Conversion Balances. Enter the trial balance as of conversion date. For each account, enter Debit or Credit. Must balance to zero. If there are outstanding invoices/bills at conversion, enter them as 'historical invoices/bills' so AR/AP aging works correctly. Common error: forgetting to enter retained earnings.

05

Files Inbox & Hubdoc

Every Xero org has a files inbox at files.xero.com. Email receipts to your unique inbox email (e.g., yourorg@xerofiles.com) and attach them to transactions. Hubdoc is included in most Xero plans — it OCRs receipts and pushes transactions to Xero automatically. Set Hubdoc to auto-publish for high-volume clients.

06

Tracking Categories

Up to 2 tracking categories per org (e.g., 'Department', 'Location'). Each category can have up to 100 options. Tracking lets you slice the P&L without bloating the COA. Use for: cost centers, regions, product lines, projects (light), branches. Cannot delete tracking history — be deliberate.

07

Dashboard & Navigation

Dashboard widgets: Bank accounts, Invoices owed to you, Bills you need to pay, Total cash in & out, Account watchlist, Expense claims. Reorder and hide widgets. Use the global search (top right) — searches contacts, transactions, accounts. Quick-add (+) button: invoice, bill, contact, expense, manual journal.

01

Sales Invoices

Business → Invoices → New Invoice. Customer must exist in Contacts (or create on the fly). Choose 'Approve' (sends to AR) or 'Approve & Email'. Branding theme controls the PDF look. Tax-inclusive vs tax-exclusive amounts — choose at line level. Send a payment receipt automatically when paid. Use 'Repeating' for recurring invoices (monthly retainers, subscriptions).

02

Quotes & Estimates

Quotes don't hit the books until accepted. Customer can accept online. Convert accepted quote to invoice in one click. Use quote templates for common services. Quote expiry date triggers a reminder.

03

Bills (Accounts Payable)

Business → Bills to Pay → New Bill. Enter supplier, due date, reference. Attach the PDF/scan from Hubdoc. Allocate to the right expense account and tax code. 'Awaiting Payment' status hits AP. Pay via Batch Payment to generate an ABA (AU), bank file, or BACS file (UK). Mark bill paid only when money has actually left the bank.

04

Bank Reconciliation — The Core Skill

When a bank line appears on the Reconcile screen, your job is to MATCH it to an existing invoice/bill OR CREATE the transaction. Match: green tick, one click. Create: type contact, account, tax code, description. Transfer: move between own bank accounts. Discuss: park it and message the client. Use Bank Rules to auto-suggest matches for recurring patterns (e.g., 'Spotify → 467 Subscriptions'). Cash Coding view lets you code dozens of similar transactions in one screen.

05

Spend Money / Receive Money

Bank account → New Transaction. Use when a payment doesn't have an invoice or bill behind it. Common cases: bank fees, interest, owner drawings (equity), reimbursements. ALWAYS pick the correct tax code — 'No GST', 'BAS Excluded', or 'No VAT' for non-business movements.

06

Contacts Management

Customers and Suppliers live in the same Contacts list — a contact can be both. Group contacts (e.g., 'VIP Clients', 'Overseas Suppliers'). Set default account/tax code per contact so future transactions auto-fill. Merge duplicates carefully — cannot be undone. Use Smart Lists to find inactive contacts.

07

Manual Journals

Accounting → Manual Journals. Use for: accruals, prepayments, depreciation, payroll journals, year-end adjustments. Must balance (debits = credits). Set as 'Show on cash basis reports' if relevant. Mark as 'Auto reverse' for accruals that flip next period. NEVER use manual journals to fix bank account balances — fix the underlying transaction instead.

01

GST Registration & Thresholds

GST registration is required when turnover hits AUD $75,000 (or $150k for not-for-profits, or any turnover for rideshare/taxi). Voluntary registration available below threshold. In Xero: Settings → Financial Settings → Tax Basis (Cash or Accruals) and GST Period (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually). Once set, it's painful to change — confirm with the client's accountant first.

02

Xero GST Tax Codes (AU)

GST on Income (10%) — sales of taxable goods/services. GST on Expenses (10%) — purchases with GST. GST Income — fresh food, medical, education, exports. GST Expenses — wages, super, bank interest, ATO payments. Input Taxed — residential rent, financial supplies. BAS Excluded — owner drawings, transfers, loan principal, depreciation. NEVER use GST on Income for owner equity contributions.

03

BAS Preparation in Xero

Reports → BAS (Activity Statement). Xero pre-fills G1 (Total sales), 1A (GST on sales), 1B (GST on purchases), W1 (Total wages), W2 (PAYG withheld), and PAYG instalments. Review the GST Reconciliation report BEFORE lodging — flags transactions with mismatched tax codes. Lodge via Xero Tax (registered agents only) or paper/ATO portal. Track 'Activity Statement' liability account against ATO payments.

04

STP Phase 2 Setup

Single Touch Payroll Phase 2 is mandatory. Payroll → Pay Items → review categories (Salary & Wages, Overtime, Allowance type, Bonuses, Directors' Fees). Each pay item must map to an ATO reporting category — Xero prompts during setup. Employee record needs: TFN, Date of Birth, Income type (Salary & Wages, Working Holiday Maker, Closely Held Payee), Tax treatment code (auto-generated).

05

Superannuation

Super Guarantee rate (current): 11.5% (rising to 12% from 1 July 2025). Calculated on Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE) — generally excludes overtime. Quarterly due dates: 28 Oct, 28 Jan, 28 Apr, 28 Jul. In Xero: Payroll → Pay Super → Auto Super (premium). Super stapling: new employees keep their existing fund unless they nominate a new one — check via ATO online services.

06

PAYG Withholding & Instalments

PAYG Withholding: tax taken from employee wages, reported in W2 of BAS. PAYG Instalments: prepaid income tax for the business itself (separate from withholding). Both appear on the BAS. Xero handles withholding automatically once tax tables are set; instalments are entered manually based on ATO notification.

07

End of Financial Year (30 June)

EOFY checklist: (1) Reconcile every bank/credit card to bank statement; (2) Review unreconciled items > 30 days; (3) Run Aged Receivables — chase or write off; (4) Run Aged Payables; (5) Reconcile payroll to STP YTD; (6) Process super up to 30 June; (7) Finalise STP — Payroll → Single Touch Payroll → Finalisation; (8) Generate Income Statements (employees access via myGov); (9) TPAR for building/cleaning/courier industries by 28 Aug; (10) Roll over inventory & lock the year.

08

Lock Dates

Settings → Advanced → Financial Settings → Lock Dates. Set 'Stop all users' to the period end after BAS is lodged. Set 'Stop adviser' to year-end after tax return is filed. Lock dates prevent retrospective edits that break lodged returns. Adviser can override their own lock to fix errors, but should re-set immediately.

01

GST Registration (NZ)

GST registration threshold: NZ$60,000 turnover in any 12-month period. Standard GST rate: 15%. Filing frequency: 6-monthly (turnover < $500k), 2-monthly (default), or monthly (turnover > $24m or by choice). Set in Xero: Financial Settings → GST Basis (Payments, Invoice, or Hybrid). Payments basis = cash basis; most small businesses choose this.

02

Xero GST Tax Codes (NZ)

15% GST on Income / 15% GST on Expenses — standard rate. Zero Rated — exports, land transactions, financial services to non-residents. GST on Imports — paid to Customs separately. No GST — wages, drawings, interest, loan principal, IRD payments. The 'GST on Income' code automatically populates Box 5/6 of the GST return.

03

GST Returns in Xero

Accounting → Reports → GST Return. Xero produces the return matching the IRD GST101A format. Review the GST Audit Report to spot misclassified transactions. File directly to IRD from Xero (if registered with myIR linked). Payment due 28th of the month following period end (or 7 May for Mar period if 2-monthly).

04

PAYE & Payroll Filing

Payday filing is mandatory: employee earnings must be reported to IRD within 2 working days of payday (electronically). In Xero: set up payroll, link to IRD via myIR, and Xero files automatically each pay run. PAYE rates use M, ME, M SL, S, SH, ST, SB codes (employee chooses on IR330). Always verify the tax code matches the IR330 on file.

05

KiwiSaver

Employer contribution: minimum 3% of gross earnings (rising context dependent). Employee can choose 3%, 4%, 6%, 8%, or 10%. Employer Superannuation Contribution Tax (ESCT) is calculated per employee based on prior year's salary + ER contribution. Xero handles ESCT automatically. KiwiSaver opt-out window: 2nd to 8th week of employment.

06

Holiday Pay (Holidays Act)

NZ Holidays Act is complex. 4 weeks annual leave after 12 months. 5 days sick leave after 6 months (rising to 10). 11 paid public holidays. Calculations: greater of Ordinary Weekly Pay (OWP) or Average Weekly Earnings (AWE) over last 52 weeks. Bereavement, family violence, parental leave separately. Xero Payroll handles this — but always check the leave balance reports monthly.

07

Year End (31 March)

NZ tax year: 1 April to 31 March. EOFY tasks: reconcile bank, run AR/AP aging, complete final GST return, process final payroll (incl. holiday pay accruals), produce financial statements. Provisional tax: paid in 3 instalments (28 Aug, 15 Jan, 7 May) for most. Terminal tax due 7 April (with tax agent) or 7 Feb (without).

08

Fringe Benefit Tax (FBT)

FBT applies to non-cash benefits to employees (motor vehicles, low-interest loans, goods, etc.). Filed quarterly or annually. Vehicle FBT calculated on cost price (default 20%) — needs a logbook to use actual business use %. In Xero: code FBT-related expenses to a dedicated tracking account so they're easy to extract for the return.

01

VAT Registration & Schemes

VAT registration threshold: £90,000 (from April 2024). Voluntary registration available below. Schemes: Standard (most common), Flat Rate (turnover < £150k, simpler reporting), Cash Accounting (turnover < £1.35m, pay VAT when received), Annual Accounting (one return + payments on account). Choose carefully — switching has rules. Xero supports all four.

02

Xero VAT Tax Codes (UK)

20% (VAT on Income/Expenses) — standard rate. 5% — reduced rate (domestic fuel, energy-saving, children's car seats). 0% — zero-rated (most food, books, children's clothing, exports outside UK). Exempt — financial services, insurance, education, healthcare. No VAT — wages, drawings, dividends, payments to HMRC. Reverse Charge — services from EU/overseas suppliers (post-Brexit). Domestic Reverse Charge — construction (CIS).

03

Making Tax Digital (MTD)

MTD for VAT is mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses. Records must be kept digitally; VAT returns must be submitted via MTD-compatible software. Xero is fully MTD-compatible. Connect: Settings → Connections → HMRC. Authorize via Gateway ID. MTD for Income Tax Self-Assessment (ITSA) starts April 2026 for sole traders/landlords > £50k income.

04

VAT Return in Xero

Accounting → Reports → VAT Return. Xero produces the 9-box return. Review with the VAT Audit Report — checks for transactions outside the period that might belong, and flags transactions with no VAT code. File directly to HMRC from Xero. VAT due 1 month and 7 days after period end (usually quarterly). Don't forget the postponed VAT accounting (PVA) statement for imports.

05

PAYE, NIC & RTI

Payroll is reported in Real Time (RTI) every payday via FPS (Full Payment Submission). Xero submits automatically when payroll is finalized. EPS (Employer Payment Summary) reports SMP, SPP, etc. Tax codes: 1257L is standard for most employees in 2024/25. NIC categories: A (standard), B (married women's reduced rate, historical), C (over state pension age), H (apprentice under 25). National Living Wage / National Minimum Wage updates each April.

06

Auto-Enrolment Pensions

Every employer must enrol eligible workers in a pension scheme. Eligible: aged 22 to state pension age, earning > £10k/year. Minimum contributions: 3% employer + 5% employee = 8% total of qualifying earnings. Xero integrates with NEST, The People's Pension, Smart Pension, etc. Re-enrolment every 3 years. Declaration of Compliance due within 5 months of staging date.

07

Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)

Contractors deduct 20% (registered subcontractor) or 30% (unregistered) from subcontractor payments. Gross status: 0% deduction (for trusted subcontractors with good record). CIS monthly return due 19th. In Xero: enable CIS in Financial Settings, mark subcontractor contacts as CIS, use CIS-specific account codes. Construction Domestic Reverse Charge applies to B2B construction services since 1 March 2021.

08

Year End (5 April)

UK tax year: 6 April to 5 April. Year-end payroll: final FPS, then 'Final submission for tax year' marker. Issue P60s to all employees by 31 May. P11D (benefits) due 6 July. Company year-end can be different — depends on incorporation date. Companies House accounts due 9 months after year-end; Corporation Tax return CT600 due 12 months after year-end (payment due 9 months + 1 day).

01

Live Bank Feeds vs Manual Statements

Live feeds ( in most regions) pull bank transactions overnight. Set up: Bank Account → Get Bank Feeds → choose bank → authorize. If feed unavailable, manually import statements as OFX/QIF/CSV. Reconcile daily for high-volume accounts; weekly is acceptable for low-volume. NEVER let reconciliation backlog exceed 30 days — patterns get hard to spot.

02

Bank Rules — Set Once, Save Hours

Bank Account → Bank Rules. Create rules for repeating transactions: 'If contains XERO SUBSCRIPTION then code to 467 Subscriptions, GST on Expenses, Contact: Xero Limited'. Rules suggest, you confirm. Use sparingly — too many rules become hard to audit. Review rule history quarterly.

03

Find & Match

When the bank shows a lump-sum deposit covering multiple invoices, click 'Find & Match', tick the invoices, save. Handles partial payments and over/underpayments. Use 'Adjustments' line for bank fees deducted from a deposit. Critical for high-volume e-commerce reconciliations (Stripe, PayPal, Square).

04

Cash Coding — Bulk Power

Bank Account → Cash Coding. Sort by description. Filter by amount, payee, date. Code 20 similar transactions in one row, then copy down. Massive time-saver for cleanup engagements. Always do Cash Coding BEFORE creating bank rules — you'll spot patterns to automate.

05

Common Reconciliation Errors & Fixes

(1) Duplicate transactions — un-reconcile both, delete one, re-reconcile. (2) Wrong tax code — un-reconcile, edit, re-reconcile (or use Find & Recode if many). (3) Coded to wrong account — same fix. (4) Bank balance doesn't match Xero — run Bank Reconciliation Summary, look at 'Unreconciled bank statement lines' and 'Outstanding payments'. (5) Old uncleared cheques — clean up annually.

06

Find & Recode (Adviser Only)

Accounting → Reports → Find & Recode. Bulk-change tax codes, accounts, contacts, tracking. Most powerful (and dangerous) tool in Xero. ALWAYS export a backup of the affected transactions before recoding. ALWAYS reconcile and lock periods after major recodes. Only available with Adviser-level access.

07

Reconciliation as a Service

Quote bank rec separately when scoping. Pricing benchmarks: AU $5–10 per account per month for clean clients; $20–30 for messy ones; flat $300–800/mo for full bookkeeping including rec. UK £4–8 per account; NZ NZ$6–12. Always demand: 1 client = 1 dedicated email folder for receipts, 1 shared drive for paperwork, 1 weekly 15-min sync.

01

Profit & Loss Variations

Standard P&L: Reports → Profit and Loss. Compare to: prior period, prior year, budget, % of revenue. Group by tracking category. Cash vs Accrual basis toggle. 'Compare Periods' for trend analysis. Always send clients the P&L showing prior-month comparison — context > snapshot.

02

Balance Sheet

Reports → Balance Sheet. Run at month-end and year-end. Reconcile each balance: bank against statement, AR against Aged Receivables, AP against Aged Payables, GST/VAT control account against return. Look for: negative bank balances (overdraft or error), suspense account balances (always investigate), shareholder loan drift.

03

Aged Receivables & Payables

Aged Receivables: who owes you, by 30/60/90/120+ days. Send statements monthly. Set Invoice Reminders (Settings → Invoice Reminders) to nudge customers automatically at 7, 14, 28 days overdue. Aged Payables: who you owe. Print before each pay run.

04

Cash Summary & Statement of Cash Flows

Cash Summary: where the money came from and went, by category. Statement of Cash Flows: the formal three-section report (Operating, Investing, Financing). Critical for businesses servicing loans or seeking finance. Many small businesses confuse profit with cash — your job is to translate.

05

Budget Manager

Accounting → Reports → Budget Manager. Enter monthly budgets per account. Import from CSV for fast entry. Track Actual vs Budget on the dashboard. Use 'Adjust by %' to scale a prior-year budget. Create multiple budgets (Base, Stretch, Cautious) and switch comparisons in reports.

06

Custom Report Layouts

Accounting → Advanced → Report Templates (Adviser only). Build branded packs: P&L by tracking, Cash Position, KPI summary, 12-month trend. Save as a Report Pack and schedule monthly. Pro bookkeeper move: send clients a 3-page Friday pack every Friday.

01

Hubdoc (Included)

Receipt and bill capture, included with most Xero plans. Email or photo → OCR → bill draft in Xero. Auto-publish for high-volume; review-then-publish for high-value. Hubdoc stores the PDF as the source document attached to the transaction.

02

Dext (formerly Receipt Bank)

Premium alternative to Hubdoc — better OCR, supplier rules, line-item extraction. Better for clients with 200+ receipts/month or complex line items (restaurants, retail). Costs ~AU$25–60/mo per client. Bill it to the client.

03

E-commerce Connectors

Shopify → A2X (gold standard) → Xero. Stripe → Stripe direct or A2X. Square → Xero direct. Amazon → A2X. NEVER pull individual transactions for high-volume e-comm — always use a settlement-based connector. Reconciliation goes from hours to minutes.

04

Inventory

Light: Xero's built-in inventory items (track on-hand for ~50 SKUs max). Mid: Cin7 Core (DEAR), Unleashed. Heavy: NetSuite, SAP (then you're out of Xero territory). Always confirm the COGS journal frequency — most inventory apps batch daily.

05

Payroll Add-ons

AU: Xero Payroll is solid for under 50 employees. KeyPay/Employment Hero for award interpretation. NZ: Xero Payroll is excellent. UK: Xero Payroll or BrightPay (more flexible).

06

Time Tracking & Project Profitability

Xero Projects (built-in, basic). WorkflowMax (Xero-owned, mid-market). Harvest, Toggl, Tsheets/QuickBooks Time. For accounting firms managing their own staff: ignore all of the above, use Karbon or XPM.

07

The Recommended Stack by Client Type

Service business < 5 staff: Xero + Hubdoc + Stripe + Xero Projects. E-commerce: Xero + A2X + Dext + Shopify/Stripe. Construction/Trades: Xero + Hubdoc + ServiceM8 + WorkflowMax. Restaurant: Xero + Dext + Lightspeed + Deputy.

01

Multi-Currency

Settings → Currencies. Add foreign currencies. Invoice/bill in foreign currency; Xero pulls daily exchange rates. Realized FX gain/loss posted automatically on payment. Unrealized FX gain/loss needs a month-end revaluation journal — Reports → Foreign Currency Gains & Losses generates the figures. Only available on Premium plans.

02

Fixed Asset Register

Accounting → Advanced → Fixed Assets. Register assets (computer, vehicle, equipment). Set depreciation method (Straight Line, Diminishing Value), useful life, % rate. Run depreciation monthly or annually. Disposal: 'Sell or Dispose' on the asset card. Disposal gain/loss posts automatically. Always reconcile the register to the fixed asset accounts on the balance sheet.

03

Xero Projects

Projects → New Project. Track time, expenses, deposits, fixed fees against a project. Quote → estimate → actual comparison. Invoice partial or full project. Best for services businesses charging by project (consulting, design, construction sub-jobs).

04

Inter-Entity & Group Companies

Each entity needs its own Xero subscription — no consolidated org. Inter-entity loans: post mirror journals in each entity. Reconcile inter-entity balances monthly. For real consolidation: export trial balances to Excel, or use Spotlight Reporting / Fathom for consolidated reporting.

05

Xero HQ & Practice Manager (For Accountants)

Xero HQ: dashboard of all client orgs, jobs, tasks. for Xero Partners. Xero Practice Manager (XPM): time billing, job management, client database. More powerful but heavier — best for 5+ staff firms. WorkflowMax is the legacy version (sunsetting).

06

Xero Tax (AU/UK/NZ)

Lodge tax returns directly. AU: BAS, individual returns, company returns, trust returns. UK: VAT returns, CT600. Linked to ATO/HMRC/IRD. Tax-agent registration required. The killer feature: client's accounts flow from Xero into Xero Tax — no re-keying.

07

Keyboard Shortcuts & Speed Tips

Global search (/ or click search icon). New invoice (top-right + → Invoice). Tab through invoice lines — Xero auto-creates a new line. Use Repeating Invoices/Bills for any monthly recurrence — saves hours/year. Use Files Inbox for clients to email receipts directly to you. Use the Xero Me mobile app for receipt photos on the go.

Common Cleanup Engagements

Most new clients arrive with a mess. Standard cleanup: (1) Run Bank Reconciliation Summary for every bank account; (2) Find & Recode incorrect tax codes in batches; (3) Re-classify suspense / uncategorised income; (4) Match historical invoices to payments via Find & Match; (5) Lock the prior period; (6) Set up bank rules to prevent future mess. Scope cleanup separately from ongoing — don't undercharge.
