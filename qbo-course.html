01

Choosing the Right QBO Subscription

Simple Start (1 user, no bills), Essentials (3 users + bills), Plus (5 users + inventory + classes/locations), Advanced (25 users + workflows). Most small business clients land on Essentials or Plus. NEVER recommend Self-Employed for true business clients — it doesn't have a proper COA. ProAdvisors get QBO Accountant edition.

02

Company Setup

Account and Settings → Company. Enter legal name, EIN (US) / Business Number (CAN), legal address, tax form (Sole Prop, LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp, Partnership, Non-Profit). Set fiscal year start. Industry — this preloads the chart of accounts. Choose the right industry on day one; switching later is messy.

03

Chart of Accounts (COA)

Accounting → Chart of Accounts. Account types in QBO: Bank, Accounts Receivable, Other Current Asset, Fixed Asset, Other Asset, Accounts Payable, Credit Card, Other Current Liability, Long-Term Liability, Equity, Income, Cost of Goods Sold, Expense, Other Income, Other Expense. Detail Type matters — it drives where the account appears on reports. Use account numbers (Settings → Advanced → Chart of Accounts → enable account numbers) for any client with > 30 accounts.

04

Users & Permissions

Settings → Manage Users. Roles: Primary Admin (one only — the owner), Company Admin (full access), Standard User (you customize), Reports Only, Time Tracking Only. Accountant Users (up to 2 per company) — invite via QBO Accountant. NEVER share login credentials with clients — always invite them with appropriate role.

05

Bank & Credit Card Connection

Banking → Link account. Search bank → enter online banking credentials. QBO downloads 90 days of history (can request up to 18 months for some banks). Set up rules to auto-categorize repeat transactions. For banks without direct connect: import CSV/QBO file manually.

06

Receipt Capture

QBO mobile app — snap a receipt, OCR extracts vendor/amount/date, attaches to a bill or expense. Or forward email receipts to a unique QBO inbox address (Banking → Receipts → Manage Forwarding Email). For high-volume: integrate Dext or Hubdoc.

07

Navigation & Quick Tips

The + (New) button is your speed weapon — invoice, expense, bill, journal entry, statement, transfer, all in one click. The Search icon (magnifier) searches transactions by amount, date, contact, memo. The Gear icon opens settings. Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Alt+I (invoice), Ctrl+Alt+W (cheque), Ctrl+Alt+E (estimate). On Mac use Cmd.

01

Sales Invoices

+ → Invoice. Choose customer, add product/service lines, set tax (if taxable). Email directly from QBO, or download PDF. Set up online payments (QBO Payments) so customers click 'Pay Now' and the deposit flows back through. Always use Products/Services list — NEVER type -text into the description field for items you sell repeatedly.

02

Sales Receipts vs Invoices

Sales Receipt = money received same day, no AR. Invoice = bill the customer now, get paid later (creates AR). Don't mix them up — recording every transaction as a Sales Receipt blows up your AR aging.

03

Estimates / Quotes

+ → Estimate. Won't post to books. Customer accepts → convert to invoice in one click. Use Estimate stages: Pending → Accepted → Closed → Rejected. Status drives reports like Open Estimates by Customer.

04

Bills (Accounts Payable)

+ → Bill. Enter vendor, terms, due date. Attach the PDF/scan. Bill Pay (QBO Bill Pay or Melio integration) lets you pay bills via ACH/check directly from QBO. Always reconcile Accounts Payable to a vendor balance summary each month.

05

Expenses vs Bills vs Cheques

Expense = card swipe/online payment, money already gone. Bill = obligation now, payment later. Cheque = paper cheque you wrote. Use the right one — picking the wrong type creates duplicate transactions when the bank feed comes in.

06

Customer & Vendor Management

Sales → Customers. Add billing/shipping address, payment terms, default tax code. Use Sub-customers for projects under one parent. Vendors: track 1099 status here (US — see Module 4). Group with Customer Types and Vendor Types for easier filtering.

07

Journal Entries

+ → Journal Entry. Use for: accruals, prepayments, depreciation, owner contributions/distributions, year-end adjustments. Must balance (debits = credits). Cannot include AR or AP accounts (use Receive Payment / Bill Payment instead). NEVER use a JE to fix a bank balance — fix the underlying transaction.

01

US Sales Tax Setup

Taxes → Sales Tax. Add agencies for each state where the client has nexus (physical presence, economic nexus thresholds, or marketplace facilitator triggers). Common nexus thresholds: $100k revenue or 200 transactions per state (post-Wayfair). Multi-state clients use a sales tax automation tool — Avalara, TaxJar — that pushes filings. QBO calculates the tax based on customer address — but you must mark products as taxable AND mark customers as taxable for the calc to happen.

02

Filing Sales Tax

Taxes → Sales Tax → Return. QBO shows what you owe per state. File and pay through the state's online portal (CA: CDTFA, NY: Tax.NY.Gov, TX: Comptroller, etc.) — or via Avalara/TaxJar. Mark return as filed in QBO so it doesn't re-include in next period. Frequency varies by state and revenue — monthly, quarterly, or annual.

03

1099-NEC Contractors

Vendors → mark vendor as 'Track payments for 1099'. Threshold: $600+ paid in a calendar year for services (not goods). Form 1099-NEC for nonemployee compensation. Form 1099-MISC for rent, royalties, other. Due to recipients by January 31. E-file via QBO's 1099 e-file service or Track1099. NEVER include payments made by credit card — those get reported on 1099-K by the processor.

04

QuickBooks Online Payroll Setup

Three QBO Payroll tiers: Core (full-service payroll, basic), Premium (HR support + same-day direct deposit), Elite (white-glove). Setup: company info, federal EIN, state withholding accounts (one per state where employees live), pay schedule, deductions (401k, HSA, health insurance pre-tax).

05

Employee Onboarding

Workers → Employees → Add Employee. Collect: SSN, address, W-4 (federal withholding), state W-4, direct deposit form, I-9 (verify work eligibility). Pay type: hourly or salary. Tax exemptions (rare). Sick/vacation accrual rules. Send the QBO Workforce invite — employees access pay stubs and W-2s themselves.

06

Running Payroll

Workers → Run Payroll. QBO pulls hours from time tracking (or enter manually). Review gross pay, taxes, deductions, net pay. Click 'Submit' — direct deposit goes 1-2 business days later (faster on Premium/Elite). QBO files federal and state taxes automatically on the auto-filed dates.

07

Year-End Payroll Tasks

By Jan 31: distribute W-2s (employees) and 1099-NECs (contractors). QBO Payroll auto-prepares W-2s — review for accuracy first. Reconcile W-2 totals to your 941 filings (quarterly). Process bonus pay runs separately so federal tax withholding applies the supplemental rate (22%) correctly.

08

State-Specific Quirks (Top 5 States)

California: SDI tax, ETT, separate SUI. Requires final paycheck on termination day. New York: NY SDI, NY PFL. Strict pay frequency rules. Texas: no state income tax — but SUI required. Florida: no state income tax — but reemployment tax. Illinois: state income tax 4.95%, monthly SUTA filings. Always check the state DOR website for current rates.

01

GST/HST Registration & Setup

Mandatory registration when revenue exceeds CAD $30,000 in any 12-month rolling period. Voluntary registration available below. In QBO: Taxes → Sales Tax → Add Tax. Default GST/HST varies by province: AB/SK/MB/BC/YT/NWT/NU = 5% GST + provincial sales tax separately; ON = 13% HST; NB/NL/NS/PEI = 15% HST; QC = 5% GST + 9.975% QST (two separate taxes).

02

Filing Frequency

Annual: revenue ≤ $1.5m. Quarterly: $1.5m–$6m. Monthly: > $6m. Most clients are annual or quarterly. Reporting period set by CRA when you register. You can request frequency changes via My Business Account.

03

Quebec QST Special Handling

QC clients must register with both CRA (GST) and Revenu Québec (QST). In QBO: enable QST tax agency. QBO calculates both taxes on each transaction. Filing happens on a single combined GST/QST return for most QC small businesses. Always confirm the QST registration number is set in Company Settings.

04

Provincial Sales Tax (PST) — BC/SK/MB

These provinces have PST (or RST in MB) ON TOP of GST. PST is generally NOT recoverable as an input tax credit — it's a true cost. Set up separate tax codes in QBO: 'GST + PST' for sales, but for purchases use 'GST only' (you can claim ITC) — and just expense the PST portion to the COGS account.

05

Filing GST/HST Return

Taxes → Sales Tax → Prepare return. QBO produces the GST34 numbers (Line 101 Sales, Line 103 GST/HST collected, Line 106 ITCs, Line 109 Net Tax). File via My Business Account on CRA. Or use QBO's 'File to CRA' integration. Always run a GST/HST detail report and review for misclassified transactions BEFORE filing.

06

T4 (Employees) Year-End

QBO Payroll (Canada) prepares T4s automatically by Feb 28. Review each employee's box 14 (employment income), 16 (CPP), 18 (EI), 22 (income tax). T4 Summary (T4SUM) submitted to CRA. Issue T4 slips to employees by end of February. Pension/RRSP contributions appear in box 20/52.

07

T4A (Contractors)

T4A for self-employed contractors paid > $500 in a year for services. Required for many service-business setups (cleaners, freelancers, subcontractors). QBO tracks T4A-flagged vendors. Box 048 'Fees for services' is the most common. Due to recipient and CRA by Feb 28.

08

Year-End Procedures (Dec 31)

Most Canadian businesses use calendar year. Steps: reconcile bank accounts, run AR/AP aging, file last GST/HST return for the year, prepare T4s/T4As, calculate corporate tax instalments, prepare year-end financials for the accountant who'll file T2 (corporate return). T2 due 6 months after year-end; tax payable 2 or 3 months after year-end depending on CCPC status.

01

QBO UK — VAT & MTD

QBO UK supports VAT registration, all major VAT schemes (Standard, Flat Rate, Cash, Annual), and Making Tax Digital (MTD). Connect to HMRC via Settings. File VAT returns directly. VAT codes match Xero: 20% standard, 5% reduced, 0% zero-rated, Exempt, No VAT, Reverse Charge. CIS support for construction is weaker than Xero — recommend Xero for CIS-heavy clients.

02

QBO Australia — GST & STP

QBO Australia handles GST, BAS reporting, and Single Touch Payroll (STP Phase 2). Tax codes: GST (10%), GST, Input Taxed, BAS Excluded. Generate BAS directly. QBO Payroll Australia covers super, PAYG withholding, leave accruals. Smaller market share than Xero — many AU accountants prefer Xero. Recommend QBO only if the client specifically asks.

03

When to Recommend Xero Instead

AU clients with complex payroll (multiple awards, casual loading) → Xero or KeyPay. UK clients with CIS or > 50 employees → Xero + BrightPay. NZ clients (almost always) → Xero (QBO doesn't support NZ as well). Multi-entity consolidations → neither, use Fathom or Spotlight Reporting on top of either.

04

Multi-Currency in QBO

Settings → Advanced → Currency → turn on. Once on, can't be turned off. Adds foreign currencies to customers/vendors/bank accounts. QBO pulls daily exchange rates. Realized FX gain/loss posts on payment. Unrealized FX (month-end) requires a manual journal — QBO doesn't auto-revalue. Available on Essentials and above.

05

Foreign Customer Invoicing

Set customer's currency on their record. Invoice in their currency. QBO converts to home currency at the invoice date rate. Payment in same currency clears the AR cleanly. Payment in different currency requires manually entering exchange rate. Always set 'Charge in foreign currency' on the customer level.

06

Accepting International Payments

QBO Payments US/CAN only. For international clients use Wise, Stripe (international), or PayPal Business. After receiving, manually create a Sales Receipt in foreign currency, or apply payment to the foreign-currency invoice.

01

The Banking Screen

Banking → For Review tab. Three buttons per transaction: Add (creates a new transaction), Match (links to existing), Find Match (advanced match across split deposits). After reviewing, click Reconcile on the bank account to formally reconcile to a statement.

02

Bank Rules

Banking → Rules. Create: 'When Description contains GOOGLE → Categorize as Software Subscription, Vendor: Google, Tax: applicable'. Auto-add option (DANGEROUS — only for 100% certain patterns). Most pros leave rules on 'suggest' not 'auto-add' so they review before posting.

03

Undeposited Funds (The Killer Account)

When you receive customer payments in QBO and don't bank them immediately, they sit in Undeposited Funds. When the bank deposit hits, use + → Bank Deposit, tick the customer payments → match the deposit. If you skip this step and just match the bank deposit directly to invoices, you create duplicate transactions. ALWAYS keep Undeposited Funds at zero at month-end.

04

Bank Reconciliation Itself

Settings → Reconcile. Enter statement ending date and balance. Tick off transactions one by one. Difference must equal zero. If it doesn't: (1) check for transactions in QBO not on statement (probably duplicates); (2) check for statement items not in QBO (missing entries); (3) check for math errors. Auto-adjust feature exists but NEVER use it — it masks real errors.

05

Common Errors & Fixes

(1) Duplicate transactions — undo the bank feed match, delete one, re-match. (2) Wrong category — re-categorize from the For Review tab if not yet added, or edit transaction directly. (3) Beginning balance off — someone changed a reconciled prior transaction; undo recent reconciliations until balance matches, then re-reconcile.

06

Credit Card Reconciliation

Same process as bank. Reconcile each month against the statement. Statement Balance to enter = ending balance from statement (the amount owed). Credits/refunds appear as deposits on the bank screen but reduce the balance on the credit card. Settle outstanding charges to ensure they appear on next month's statement.

01

Profit & Loss

Reports → Profit and Loss. Default is Accrual basis — toggle to Cash if needed. Compare: prior period, prior year, % of income, % expense. Group by classes or locations (Plus+). Customize columns (Months, Quarters, Customers). Save Customization → schedule email delivery.

02

Balance Sheet

Reports → Balance Sheet. Always reconcile each balance: bank to statement, AR to AR aging, AP to AP aging, sales tax payable to sales tax return. Look for: negative balances on bank (overdraft or error), Undeposited Funds > 0 (un-deposited customer payments), Opening Balance Equity > 0 (always investigate — should be zero after setup).

03

AR & AP Aging

Reports → Customers & Receivables → A/R Aging Summary / Detail. Send statements monthly. Reports → Vendors & Payables → A/P Aging Summary / Detail. Print before each bill payment run. Auto-send statements: Sales → All Sales → Statements → set up automation.

04

Cash Flow & Budget

Reports → Statement of Cash Flows (3 sections: Operating, Investing, Financing). Reports → Budget Overview if budget set up (Settings → Budgeting). Budgets in QBO Plus+ allow Actual vs Budget comparison. Pro tip: enter monthly budgets per account, copy prior year + 10% growth as starting point, then refine.

05

Custom Report Groups

Reports → Custom Reports. Save customized reports here. Group into Report Groups (e.g., 'Monthly Owner Pack' = P&L + Balance Sheet + Cash Flow + AR Aging). Schedule the whole group via email to the client on the 5th of each month. Massive time-saver.

06

Classes & Locations (Plus+)

Settings → Advanced → Categories. Turn on Classes (functions: department, product line) and Locations (regions, branches). Tag every transaction. Run P&L by Class for segment performance. Don't enable both unless you really need both — adds friction to every entry.

07

QBO Advanced — Workflows & Custom Reports

Workflows automate: 'send reminder when invoice is X days overdue', 'notify accountant when expense > $5,000'. Custom Fields on transactions and contacts. Custom Roles for granular permissions. Worth the upgrade only for clients with > 15 employees or > $5m revenue.

01

Receipt & Bill Automation

Hubdoc ( with subscription on some plans). Dext (formerly Receipt Bank) — premium OCR. Bill.com — full AP workflow with approvals. AutoEntry. Most US bookkeepers use Hubdoc OR Dext. For clients with approval flows or > $50k/mo in bills: Bill.com.

02

Payments & Invoicing

QBO Payments (US/CAN — credit card 2.9% + 25¢; ACH 1% capped). Stripe (alternative, often cheaper for online businesses). Square (POS-heavy). PayPal (consumer-friendly). For B2B services: ACH via QBO Payments wins.

03

E-commerce

Shopify + A2X (gold standard). Amazon + A2X. Stripe + A2X. eBay + A2X. WooCommerce + Webgility. NEVER pull individual transactions from a high-volume store — use a settlement-based connector and reconcile to the daily/weekly payout.

04

Inventory Management

Built-in QBO inventory (basic, FIFO only). For more: SOS Inventory, Cin7 Core (DEAR), Fishbowl, SkuVault. Pick based on units sold and SKU count. Most small clients can live with built-in. Mid-market and up needs a dedicated inventory app.

05

Time Tracking & Payroll

QuickBooks Time (formerly TSheets) — integrates seamlessly with QBO Payroll. Gusto — alternative payroll provider, popular for tech companies, integrates with QBO. Hubstaff — basic time tracking for remote teams. Deputy — scheduling-heavy.

06

CRM & Sales

HubSpot ( CRM tier, integrates with QBO). Salesforce (enterprise, complex). Method:CRM (built ON TOP of QBO). For service businesses with < 20 staff: HubSpot + QBO works great.

07

The Recommended Stack by Client Type

Service business < 5 staff: QBO Essentials + Hubdoc + QBO Payments + QBO Payroll. E-commerce: QBO Plus + A2X + Dext + Stripe. Construction: QBO Plus + Knowify + QuickBooks Time. Real estate (US): QBO Plus + Stessa or AppFolio.

01

The Cleanup Diagnostic

Step 1: Run Reconciliation Discrepancy report — flags every period where ending balance doesn't match. Step 2: Run Audit Log — see who changed what. Step 3: Run Trial Balance — look for negative balances, suspense accounts, unusual entries. Step 4: Run AR + AP Aging — look for stale invoices/bills > 1 year. Step 5: Run Opening Balance Equity report — should be zero. If not, someone messed up setup.

02

The Cleanup Workflow

(1) Get bank statements for the full period; (2) Reconcile each month sequentially — DO NOT skip; (3) Fix duplicate transactions; (4) Re-categorize misclassified items in batches via Banking → For Review or in Chart of Accounts → Register; (5) Close Undeposited Funds; (6) Resolve Opening Balance Equity; (7) Run final reports and document changes in Audit Log.

03

Reclassify Transactions Tool (QBOA only)

Accountant Tools → Reclassify Transactions. Bulk-change account, class, or location on multiple transactions at once. Massive cleanup time-saver. Only available in QBO Accountant — get the Accountant subscription if you don't have it.

04

Year-End Close Checklist

(1) Final reconciliation of all bank and credit card accounts; (2) Send all customer statements; (3) Process final payroll, file Q4 941; (4) Issue 1099s (US) / T4s (CAN) by Jan 31 / Feb 28; (5) File final sales tax returns; (6) Adjust journal entries for depreciation, accruals, deferred revenue; (7) Close the books — Settings → Advanced → Accounting → Close the books; (8) Send year-end financials to the CPA.

05

Closing the Books

Setting a close date prevents retroactive edits to prior periods. Set close date password. Adviser/Accountant can override with password to fix errors; immediately re-close after. NEVER skip this step — it's how you keep prior-year reports stable for tax filing.

06

Working With the CPA

QBO has an 'Accountant's Copy' feature — send a snapshot to the CPA who works in it while you continue in the live file. CPA returns 'accountant's changes' which merge in. Alternative: invite CPA as Accountant User. Most CPAs prefer Accountant User access. Always provide: trial balance, all bank statements, full reconciliation reports, AR/AP aging, depreciation schedule, payroll summary.

US Case 1 — 'Lone Star Apparel' (Texas E-commerce LLC)

02

US Case 2 — 'Florida Build Co' (S-Corp Contractor)

Setup: QBO Plus + QuickBooks Time, 8 W-2 employees + 6 1099 subs, QBO Payroll Premium. Monthly tasks: (1) job costing by project (using Classes for projects); (2) reconcile separate operating + tax savings accounts; (3) reconcile owner's reasonable salary calculation; (4) 1099 tracking for subs ($600 threshold per sub per year); (5) FL reemployment tax filings; (6) Schedule K-1 prep at year end. Pricing: $950/mo.

03

CAN Case — 'Toronto Consulting Inc' (Ontario CCPC)

Setup: QBO Essentials + Wagepoint payroll (2 employees), HST quarterly filer. Monthly tasks: (1) reconcile CIBC bank + Visa card; (2) review WIP and unbilled hours; (3) Wagepoint payroll runs; (4) HST quarterly return prep; (5) shareholder loan account reconciliation (very common in Canadian CCPCs); (6) year-end T4 + T2 prep handoff. Pricing: CAD $700/mo.

04

Standard Cleanup Engagement Scope

Most new clients arrive 6-12 months behind. Scope of cleanup: agreed period (e.g., last 12 months), all bank/credit card accounts reconciled, AR/AP corrected, COA cleaned, sales tax returns reviewed, payroll reconciled to filings. Quote SEPARATELY from ongoing bookkeeping. Typical cleanup price: US $50-150/hr × estimated hours, OR flat $1,500-$5,000 depending on volume and mess level.

05

Pricing Your QBO Services (USD)

Setup fee: $400-1,500 (one-time). Monthly bookkeeping: $300-1,500 based on transaction count + complexity. Sales tax filing: $100-300 per state per period. Payroll: $100-500/mo (depending on # employees). 1099 issuance: $25-50 per 1099. Always quote a setup fee AND monthly retainer — never just hourly. Hourly traps you in trading hours for dollars; flat-fee scales.
