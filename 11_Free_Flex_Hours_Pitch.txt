================================================================
FREE FLEXIBLE-HOURS PITCH SCRIPT
For bookkeepers asking a client (or prospect) to agree to a
flexible / async / non-traditional working schedule.
================================================================

WHEN TO USE THESE SCRIPTS:
- During an Upwork / OLJ proposal
- In a discovery call before signing the engagement
- In a kickoff email after a client says yes
- When renegotiating with an existing client
- When applying to a job post that says "must be available 9-5 EST"

THE BIG IDEA:
Don't apologize for needing flexible hours. Frame your async-first,
deep-work style as the BENEFIT the client is buying. US/AU clients
have been burned by VAs who go quiet — what they actually want is
predictable communication and on-time deliverables. Give them that
and the schedule stops mattering.

================================================================
DON'T SAY                          SAY INSTEAD
================================================================
"I can only work nights"        →  "I work my deep-focus block from
                                    9 PM to 1 AM PHT, which overlaps
                                    your morning."

"I have kids so I can't…"       →  "I run my practice in 4-hour
                                    blocks, Monday to Friday."

"I can't always be online"      →  "I'm async-first — I batch all
                                    questions into one daily Loom."

"My schedule is unpredictable"  →  "My schedule is fixed. Here are
                                    my work blocks for the week."

"I'm just starting out"         →  "I keep my client list small so
                                    every client gets real attention."

SCRIPT #1 — UPWORK / OLJ PROPOSAL (the opener that wins flex jobs)
================================================================

Hi [Client First Name],

I noticed your job mentions [specific detail from the post]. Quick
note about how I work, so we're aligned from the start:

• I run a small remote practice, so every client gets focused
  attention — not "VA team" treatment.
• My work pattern is async-first: I deliver via Loom walkthroughs,
  daily Slack updates, and a weekly written status email. You
  never have to wonder where the books stand.
• My live-meeting block is [Tuesday + Thursday, 9–11 PM PHT] —
  that's [6–8 AM your time]. We can do a 30-min weekly call here.
• My deep-work block is [9 PM – 1 AM PHT] = [your morning],
  Monday to Friday. That's when reconciliations, AR/AP, and the
  close happen.
• I commit to a 12-hour response time, business days.

If that working rhythm fits, I'd love to talk. I can start with a
2-hour paid trial so you can evaluate the experience risk-free.

[Your Name] · QB ProAdvisor · Xero Advisor

SCRIPT #2 — DISCOVERY CALL (when the client says "I need someone full-time 9-5")
================================================================

Totally hear you — and honestly, "9 to 5 in your time zone" is the
default ask. Can I tell you what most of my clients actually find
works better?

Bookkeeping is async work. 90% of what I do — reconciling, categorizing,
chasing receipts, building the P&L — doesn't need me online at the
same time as you. What you actually need from your bookkeeper is:

  1. Predictable monthly close, hit on the same day every month.
  2. Fast, written replies — never voicemail roulette.
  3. Clean handoffs before any deadline.
  4. A 30-minute live check-in once a week or month.

I deliver all four. My deep-focus hours happen to be [time block],
which means when YOU log in tomorrow morning, your inbox already
has my Loom walkthrough, the reconciled bank feed, and three
clarifying questions answered.

Would you be open to trying that rhythm for 30 days, and we can
reset if it isn't working for you?

  • Deep-focus block: 9 PM – 1 AM PHT, Mon–Fri (your AM)
  • Reply window: within 12 hours on business days
  • Anything that comes in on weekends → I'll reply Monday morning
  • Truly urgent only? Text "URGENT" and I'll respond same-day

Would this work for you? Happy to talk it through on Tuesday's call.

================================================================
MARY'S RULES FOR PITCHING FLEX HOURS
================================================================

1. State your working blocks UP FRONT. Hiding them = anxious clients.
2. Use the word "rhythm" not "schedule" — softer and more inviting.
3. Always pair your flex hours with a commitment (response time +
   weekly written status + monthly close cadence).
4. Offer a 30-day trial period. Lowers risk, wins the close.
5. Reframe overnight work as a BENEFIT ("while you sleep…").
6. If a client keeps pushing back, they're not your client. Walk
   away with grace — there are plenty of clients who PREFER async.
