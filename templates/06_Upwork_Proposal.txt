================================================================
UPWORK PROPOSAL TEMPLATE — REMOTE BOOKKEEPER
Use this for every Upwork bookkeeping job · Customize per job
================================================================

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Read the WHOLE job post twice before writing your proposal.
- Identify ONE specific detail to reference in the first line.
- Keep total length under 200 words.
- Always end with a question or a clear CTA.

================================================================
THE TEMPLATE
================================================================

Hi [Client First Name],

I noticed your job mentions [SPECIFIC DETAIL from the post — e.g.,
"Shopify reconciliation" or "8 months of QBO cleanup" or "monthly
retainer for a small e-commerce store"]. I have handled this exact
scenario [N] times over the past [X] years.

A bit about me:
- Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor + Xero Advisor
- Based in the Philippines, full-time available
- Time-zone overlap with your [PST / EST / AEST / GMT] hours: [time block]
- Worked with [type of clients you've served]

My plan for your project:
1. [Step 1 — show you read their job, e.g., "Pull the last 3 months of bank statements and reconcile to QBO."]
2. [Step 2 — e.g., "Categorize all uncategorized transactions; flag anything I'd need your input on in one consolidated message."]
3. [Step 3 — e.g., "Deliver a clean Trial Balance + revised P&L and BS by [date]."]
4. [Step 4 — e.g., "Set up bank rules to reduce future cleanup time."]

Estimated time: [N] hours. I'm happy to start with a 2-hour paid trial so you can evaluate my work risk-free.

Quick question: [ASK ONE thoughtful question that proves you understood the job — e.g., "Are you on QBO Plus or Essentials? That changes how I'd set up the bank rules."]

Thanks for considering me,
[Your First Name]
QB ProAdvisor #[ID] · Xero Advisor Certified
Portfolio: [link]
Loom intro: [link]

THE LOOM TRICK (huge conversion boost)
================================================================

For every proposal on a job that pays $20+/hour, record a 60-90
second Loom video:

- Camera on, face visible, smiling
- Say the client's name and the business name
- Mention ONE specific thing from their job post
- Walk through ONE screenshot of your sample work
- End with: "I'd love to chat. Available [time block]. Have a great day!"

Paste the Loom link at the bottom of your proposal. This single
tactic 3-4x'd my response rate when I started.

================================================================
DOs and DON'Ts
================================================================

✅ DO
- Read the whole job post twice
- Mention ONE specific detail in line 1
- Keep it under 200 words
- Quote a fair price for YOUR experience level
- Offer a small paid trial
- Attach Loom + portfolio + cert links

❌ DON'T
- Use a generic "Dear Sir/Madam" opener
- Copy-paste the same proposal everywhere
- Quote the lowest possible rate (you'll be ignored)
- Promise unrealistic timelines
- Mention your other clients' details by name
- Apologize for being from the Philippines (don't!)
